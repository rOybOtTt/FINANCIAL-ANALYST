      Method and gaps

      Sources, tags and what we could not find

      Tags. F = company document or exchange price data. A = attributed to press, aggregators or brokers, not independently verified. E = our arithmetic. Most 2026 operating readings are A; Murata's book-to-bill, utilization and fact-book series are F.

      Score. Twelve signals, weights 1-3 (total 27). Score = sum of (status × weight) ÷ (2 × total weight). Re-score after each release in the checklist.

      Backtest. Equal-weight, monthly-rebalanced basket of Murata, SEMCO, Taiyo Yuden, Yageo and Walsin, from Yahoo adjusted closes in local currency; peaks from a 25-month centred window. Six completed cycles is a small sample.

      Files. Research memos, CSVs and build scripts live in the repo under targets/MLCC.

      Gaps
      • No primary capacity data in bn pcs/month for any maker; shipments are a proxy.
      • Paumanok and TrendForce MLCC price tables are paywalled; price actions come from press reports.
      • Murata's MLCC price-hike letter is not confirmed from a primary source.
      • No 2004-2010 MLCC price, utilization or inventory data; those cycles rely on stock prices.
      • Channel inventory definitions conflict (TrendForce under 30 days vs Yageo 2-6 months).
      • Three-Circle's 100bn pcs/month claim rests on a single unverified source.
