◆Overview of the Incident
     On February 28 2026, Murata became aware of the possibility of unauthorized access and initiated an initial investigation, and
      on March 1, a full-scale investigation was commenced.
     As a result of these investigations, Murata confirmed that unauthorized access had occurred and that data was improperly
      accessed.
     Murata has completed its immediate response to the unauthorized access, and to date, no new damage or impact on business
      operations has been identified.

                • Murata’s systems are operating normally, and Murata’s production and sales activities have not been affected.

Revenue     1,960.0 billion yen
• Achieved record-high sales for the second consecutive year. Revenue increased by ＋7.1％ year on year, on the
  back of stronger data-center-related demand, mainly for capacitors.
Operating profit     380.0 billion yen
• Operating profit were up 34.8 % year-on-year, gains in capacity utilization from higher production output driven by

  despite price declines and higher fixed costs.

• Total investment is planned to remain at the same level as the previous fiscal year. Although investments in land

  data centers.(Includes approx. 40 billion out of approx. 80 billion in additional production investment.)
