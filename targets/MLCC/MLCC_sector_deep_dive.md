## §1 Sector map and value chain

- **Upstream:**
  - Barium-titanate powder: Murata and Samsung in-house; Sakai Chemical ~25-28%, Fuji Titanium [A].
  - Nickel and copper paste; furnaces and printers.
- **Makers:** the seven listed names plus Chinese entrants.
- **Downstream:**
  - Distributors (about a third of volume, and where double-ordering builds).
  - AI-server ODMs, handset OEMs, and auto Tier-1s.
- **Chokepoint:** high-cap, small-case parts for GPU power. Murata and SEMCO hold 80-85% [A].
- **Proposed additions:** Fenghua and Three-Circle as supply-risk names, plus Samwha (Korea) if data is found. We would not hold them; we track them as the capacity response (SC12).

## §3 Macro and cycle

The three variables that drive the sector:

1. **Hyperscaler capex.** Over $700bn in 2026, raised through the year. It leads MLCC orders by 1-3 quarters.
2. **Consumer electronics units.** About half of MLCC units; demand is "subdued".
3. **Channel inventory**, which is where every bust starts:
   - Dec-2018: 6-7 months in distribution.
   - Today: under 30 days per TrendForce, but 2-6 months by Yageo's channel definitions.

**Murata capacitor book-to-bill** is the single best cycle gauge:

| Period | Reading |
|---|---|
| FY2019 Q1 (2019 trough) | 0.80 |
| 2021 peak | 1.32 |
| FY2022 Q3 (2022 trough) | 0.79 |
| Apr-Jun 2026 (MLCC) | 1.47 |

Each time, the ratio fell below 1 two quarters before the order trough [F].

## §4 Competitive landscape

- **AI leaders:** Murata and SEMCO. Taiyo Yuden, TDK, Yageo and Kyocera each have under 5% of AI-server share.
- **Cycle levers:** Taiyo Yuden (69% capacitors), Yageo and Walsin (~45%). They have the highest beta to MLCC price and the deepest busts (-68% to -89%).
- **Diversified:** TDK (batteries are 55% of sales) and Kyocera (packaging, KDDI stake).
- **Who is winning at whose expense:**
  - SEMCO has closed to within ~5 points of Murata in AI servers.
  - Yageo leads on price.
  - Chinese makers take commodity share and are moving into high-cap parts (SC12).
  - The TDK-Taiyo Yuden alliance (29 Sep 2026) could create a #3 in high-cap parts.
