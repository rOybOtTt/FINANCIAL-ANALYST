1. 2025 market ~USD 27bn (Mordor), 2024 range 14.5-24bn; definitions vary.
2. 2024 units ~3.6 trillion pcs (verify).
3. Murata ~40% global, ~45% AI-server share; SEMCO ~18% / ~40%.
4. Consumer electronics ~51% of revenue; auto ~26%, fastest growing.
5. AI server ~8x conventional server MLCC content (Murata).
6. GB200 NVL72 rack ~440k MLCCs; board 6.5k (GB200) vs 12k (Rubin).
7. ICE ~3,000 vs BEV 10,000-18,000.
8. Murata +15-35% AI/auto price hike from 2026-04-01.
9. Lead times 20-26 wks (vs 8-10); book-to-bill ~1.30 vs 2018 peak 1.25.
10. AI MLCC ~30% CAGR to 2030 (Murata); channel inventory 1-1.5 months.
